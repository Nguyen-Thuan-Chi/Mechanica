# Art Bible

Original illustrated mechanical adventure, front-facing, aged tactile metal, painterly material detail, upper-left key light, deep shadows and selective luminous accents. No reference pixels shipped.

CH01: amber brass, dark walnut, rust and steam. CH02: verdigris copper, wet glass, water and moss. CH03: obsidian, silver, indigo, cyan crystal and starlight. CH04: charred titanium, red-orange reactor and white energy.

Rooms: 16:9, environment without embedded puzzle UI, calm central working space. Device art: orthographic, alpha, centered pivot; dynamic state rendered separately. Texture layers, pipes, shafts, gauges, liquid, optical rays and particles are independent runtime layers. Labels are Vietnamese runtime text. Buttons use engraved metal borders, serif display titles and legible sans body.

Stable assets: ART_CH01_ROOM…ART_CH04_ROOM, ART_CH01_KIT…ART_CH04_KIT. Each kit is a single normalized 2×2 production atlas: device, architectural prop, foreground ornament, signature light source. Four independent crops are placed/animated separately. Original procedural auxiliaries supplement, never replace, illustrated room art.

Art gate requires actual image files, alpha inspection, four Android screenshots and readable interactive devices. Commercial polish remains a visual judgment, not a test-count claim.
